### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a highly motivated **Computer Science & Engineering B.Tech student (Class of 2028)** at Ramgarh Engineering College and an active core member of **Team Udaan**. I specialize in developing high-performance full-stack web applications, immersive interactive UI interfaces, native Android apps, and securing systems.
